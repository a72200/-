7Worlds Empire | Global Booking Platform & Holographic Real-World Destination
Tagline: We Anticipate the Future. We Build It.
Project status: Concept / early-stage prototype
Founder: Ahmed Amin
Project name: 7Worlds Empire
Core vision: Build a global booking platform that connects people with travel, stays, experiences, events, education, entertainment, and future smart-destination services, while developing a real-world destination powered by immersive holographic and AI-enabled experiences.
1. Overview
7Worlds Empire is envisioned as two connected products:
A global digital booking platform where users can discover, compare, and reserve participating hotels, travel services, attractions, events, learning experiences, and destination activities.
A real-world holographic destination designed around immersive environments, interactive storytelling, smart services, entertainment, hospitality, education, and innovation.
The digital platform is the practical starting point. It can launch as a focused booking marketplace and expand over time through verified partners, new booking categories, and destination pilots. The physical destination is a long-term development vision that would require feasibility studies, land and infrastructure planning, technical validation, regulatory approvals, and strategic investment.
2. Mission
Make discovering and booking meaningful experiences easier, more connected, and more immersive, while creating a pathway toward a new generation of intelligent destinations.
3. The Two-Part Ecosystem
A. Global Booking Platform
The platform is intended to help travelers and local users discover and book services through one digital experience.
Potential categories
Hotels, resorts, and distinctive stays
Tours, attractions, and destination activities
Events, festivals, and entertainment
Restaurants and dining experiences
Educational programs, workshops, and innovation experiences
Transport and travel add-ons, where supported by partners
Future bookings for 7Worlds destination experiences
Core user journey
Search by destination, date, category, budget, and preferences.
Compare available options and clear booking terms.
View partner profiles, photos, amenities, accessibility details, and policies.
Submit a booking request or reserve through an integrated partner system.
Receive confirmation, itinerary details, reminders, and support.
Manage changes or cancellations according to the provider's policy.
Leave a review after the experience, subject to platform rules.
Potential platform features
Arabic and English at launch, with additional languages later
Responsive website first, followed by mobile apps if justified
User accounts, saved favorites, booking history, and notifications
Partner dashboards for listings, availability, pricing, and booking management
Secure payment integration through approved providers
AI-assisted recommendations and multilingual customer support
Accessibility filters and transparent cancellation terms
Reviews, fraud prevention, privacy controls, and customer-support workflows
Analytics for partners and platform operators
Important launch principle: Begin with a manageable category and region, validate demand, and expand only when supply, support, and booking operations are reliable. The platform should not claim worldwide inventory until real partner coverage exists.
B. Holographic Real-World Destination
The physical concept is a future-facing destination combining hospitality, learning, entertainment, production, and interactive technology.
Concept elements
Immersive holographic environments and projection-based experiences
AI-guided visitor journeys and interactive storytelling
Smart hotel and hospitality services
Digital city experiences and connected destination services
Educational and innovation spaces
Live events, media production, games, and creative festivals
Smart gardens, themed zones, and experiential public spaces
Renewable-energy integration where technically and financially feasible
Multilingual guest assistance and accessible visitor routes
Holographic experience approach The term holographic may describe several technologies, including projection mapping, transparent displays, spatial displays, augmented reality, mixed reality, and volumetric displays. Each proposed experience should be matched to the appropriate technology after testing. The project should avoid implying that every concept can be delivered by a single technology or that all components are already built.
Development pathway
Create a digital concept model and visitor journey.
Build a small proof of concept for one holographic or spatial experience.
Test usability, safety, operating costs, and audience interest.
Develop a pilot attraction or partner-hosted demonstration.
Conduct site, infrastructure, environmental, legal, and financial feasibility studies.
Secure qualified operators, technology suppliers, development partners, and investment.
Expand in phases based on verified results.
4. How the Digital Platform Connects to the Physical Destination
The booking platform can serve as the digital front door to the future destination.
Visitors discover experiences and reserve tickets or stays.
Guests receive itineraries, maps, accessibility information, and updates.
AI assistance can help users navigate available services and understand options.
Partner operators manage inventory and service details through their own dashboards.
Usage data, collected with appropriate consent and privacy protections, can help improve services.
The physical destination can be introduced as a pilot experience before a larger site is developed.
This creates a practical bridge between an early-stage digital product and a longer-term physical development vision.

6. Business Model
Potential revenue streams, subject to market validation and local regulations:
Commission on completed bookings
Partner listing or software tools, if useful to providers
Service fees disclosed clearly before checkout
Corporate and group booking services
Ticketing and packages for events or experiences
Licensing or operating partnerships for destination technology
Future ticket, hospitality, and experience revenue from a physical destination
The first version should test one or two revenue streams rather than relying on every possible source at once.
7. Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
Phase 1: Booking marketplace prototype
Landing page explaining the value proposition
Search and category pages
A small set of verified pilot listings
Listing detail pages with dates, prices, availability, and policies
Booking request or partner checkout flow
User contact and support process
Basic partner administration
Privacy policy, terms, and cancellation information
Phase 2: Operational validation
Test with a small group of providers and users
Measure listing-to-booking conversion, successful bookings, cancellations, support response time, and repeat usage
Fix trust, inventory, payment, and support problems before expanding
Phase 3: Intelligent features
Personalized discovery with user consent
Multilingual AI assistance with human escalation
Partner analytics and operational tools
Carefully tested integrations with external booking and payment systems
Phase 4: Holographic pilot
Demonstrate one clearly scoped immersive experience
Gather audience feedback and technical performance data
Use results to support a feasibility study and partner discussions
8. Trust, Safety, and Operations
Verify participating providers and their authority to sell the listed service.
Display accurate prices, taxes, fees, availability, and cancellation terms.
Use established payment providers; do not store raw payment-card details.
Protect personal data and collect only what is necessary.
Provide a clear process for refunds, disputes, complaints, and urgent support.
Follow applicable tourism, consumer-protection, accessibility, privacy, tax, and business regulations.
Obtain appropriate permissions and safety reviews for physical attractions and immersive installations.
Clearly distinguish proposed features from working features and signed partnerships.
9. Early Success Metrics
For the booking platform:
Number of verified active providers
Number of live bookable listings
Search-to-listing and listing-to-booking conversion
Completed bookings and cancellation rate
Customer-support response time
Repeat booking rate and user satisfaction
Provider retention and provider satisfaction
For the holographic pilot:
Successful completion of the experience
Visitor satisfaction and accessibility feedback
Technical uptime and incident rate
Cost per visitor and operating requirements
Partner interest and willingness to support a larger pilot
10. Key Risks and Mitigation
Risk
Mitigation
Competing with established booking platforms
Start with a focused niche, local supply, or distinctive experiences
Insufficient partner inventory
Recruit and verify a small pilot group before public launch
Booking errors or outdated availability
Use reliable partner integrations or clearly label request-based bookings
User trust and refunds
Publish transparent policies and provide responsive support
Holographic technology costs or limitations
Prototype one experience and validate technology before scaling
Large physical-development cost
Separate the digital MVP from the destination investment plan and develop in stages
Overly broad initial scope
Prioritize a small, measurable launch and add categories based on evidence
11. Roadmap
Stage 1: Define and validate
Select the initial market and first booking category
Interview prospective users and providers
Confirm the legal and operational model
Build a simple prototype and recruit pilot partners
Stage 2: Launch a controlled pilot
Publish verified listings
Test booking, customer support, and cancellation workflows
Track the first success metrics
Improve reliability and conversion
Stage 3: Expand the marketplace
Add providers and categories based on demand
Introduce partner tools and supported payment integrations
Add multilingual and AI-assisted features where they provide measurable value
Stage 4: Demonstrate the physical vision
Build a digital 3D visitor model
Prototype a single holographic or spatial experience
Identify technology and destination-development partners
Commission feasibility and cost studies before site commitments
12. Partnership and Investment Opportunity
7Worlds Empire is seeking conversations with:
Travel and hospitality providers for the booking-platform pilot
Technology partners for AI, immersive displays, projection mapping, AR/VR, and visitor systems
Destination operators and developers for feasibility and pilot planning
Strategic investors and sponsors aligned with staged, evidence-based development
The immediate opportunity is to validate and build the digital booking product. The holographic destination is a connected long-term vision that should advance through prototypes, feasibility work, and qualified partnerships.
